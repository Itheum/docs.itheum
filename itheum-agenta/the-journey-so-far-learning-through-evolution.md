# The Journey So Far - Learning Through Evolution

Since its inception in 2018, Itheum has constantly evolved through industry cycles, guided by a single goal: to empower people and organizations to own, control, and trade their data. Each version of Itheum was a reflection of the technological and cultural moment - and each brought lessons that shaped the next.

### V0 (2018): Web2 Health Data Ownership

Our first version tackled healthcare data ownership, letting patients collect and control their data in encrypted Web2 stores. We learned that while healthcare was ripe for disruption, Web2 ecosystems resist data ownership - control was deeply institutional.

### V1 (2022): Data DEX and the Metaverse Narrative

We launched a Data DEX and Data NFTs, allowing creative and professional data assets to be traded. The idea resonated in the early Web3 world, winning/placing in multiple hackathons (Polygon, Hedera, FileCoin, Ivan on Tech’s web3 hackathon etc) and partnerships. Yet, as the NFT and metaverse narratives cooled, we realized timing and user adoption were everything.

### V2 (2024): Data for the AI Era

We pivoted to “Bulk Data” Data NFTs for AI training, gaming, and music - alongside NFMe ID and Liveliness Staking for proof of humanity. Our tech was recognized globally (Sony Web3 Accelerator finalist, Solana Summit hackathon top 5 winner). But as decentralized AI hype faded, adoption slowed.

### V3 (2024): Hyper-Digital AGI-Ready Data

As we observed the growing volume of data being produced and consumed, alongside the rise of agentic AI, we pivoted to a **full-stack abstraction and unification layer** for tokenization, storage, encryption, and IP licensing. This enabled data to move freely between people, platforms, and intelligent systems while preserving ownership and trust.

We built an **Enterprise Gateway** that allowed organizations and individuals to create tokenized data through a simple click-and-drag experience and paying using tradional payments rails (Credit Cards - not crypto), with built-in storage, encryption, and IP protection. We saw success with AI-remixable, IP-protected tokenized music through our partner app, [Sigma Music](https://sigma.fans/).

However, we realized that AI companies had already scraped much of the world’s IP and data multiple times over and were not interested in procuring new forms of data (as they already had it all!). This raised a more important question: how can we trust anyone or anything we see today?

### Key Learnings

Across all versions, we learned the vital importance of timing, market fit, and cultural readiness. The idea of data ownership and human-centric trust is powerful, but to find true product-market fit, it must align with where the world is heading.
